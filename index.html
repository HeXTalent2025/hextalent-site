The founder

Freedom over status.

I'm Jeff. I played Aussie rules from the age of five and represented Queensland as a teenager. Then I found singing, and spent my twenties touring the world, finishing with musical theatre for Opera Australia and a children's play we took to regional towns in every state and territory.

At 30 I moved to Melbourne and started again in sales and business development. That led me into HR recruitment, and during COVID I took a gamble and founded my own recruitment company.

When our kids came along, we moved home to the Sunshine Coast. Now I run a small portfolio: I clean houses, run a small HR search practice, and build with AI. Cleaning gives me freedom, people and time on the road, and AI agents build my products while I work. Recruitment is where a decade of expertise pays off. AI is what I'm building towards.

Every chapter has been the same decision: freedom over status.

Careers in HR

A connected ecosystem for Australian HR professionals: HR market visibility, a role consideration workspace, profile-led career positioning and private in-role reflection, all under one annual membership.

Market shows what's happening. Read helps members think through specific roles. Talent strengthens how they position themselves. Tenure helps them understand the career story they're already building.

Market

The member-only view of the Australian HR opportunity market: live employer and recruiter roles, categories, locations, Role Radar insights and featured opportunities in one HR-specific place.

market.careersinhr.com.au

Read

The role consideration workspace. Save roles from Market, keep source links and job context together, and create ReadNotes that show how your CV, cover letter and experience line up against a specific opportunity.

read.careersinhr.com.au

Talent

The career positioning layer. Clarify the roles you want next, build a stronger private profile, and find better language for your strengths, motivations, experience and direction, connected to aligned opportunities across Careers in HR.

talent.careersinhr.com.au

Tenure

The private in-role reflection space. Capture what's happening while you're still in a role (fit, friction, confidence, growth, evidence and lessons) and turn day-to-day work into clearer career insight over time.

Sunny Home

An AI memory for your home. For homeowners tired of carrying the whole house in their head. Send Sunny anything to do with your home (a receipt, a warranty, a quote, a photo, a passing thought) and it remembers, works out what it is, and hands it back the moment you ask.

  • Send it your way: forward an email, upload a file or jot a quick note. No folders, no tags.
  • Ask in plain English, like "Who serviced the air con?", and get it back in seconds.
  • Private by default: your home's memory is yours, never sold and never used to train AI.
Visit sunnyhome.com.au
