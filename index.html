Careers in HR

Careers in HR Australia has developed into a connected ecosystem for Australian HR professionals — bringing together HR market visibility, a role consideration workspace, profile-led career positioning, and private in-role reflection. One annual membership unlocks all four surfaces.

Market shows what's happening. Read helps members think through specific roles. Talent strengthens how they position themselves. Tenure helps them understand the career story they're already building.

Market

The member-only intelligence layer for the Australian HR opportunity market — live employer-led roles, recruiter-led signals, categories, locations, Role Radar insights, and featured opportunities, all in one HR-specific environment.

market.careersinhr.com.au

Read

The role consideration workspace — a place to save roles from Market, keep source links and job context together, and create ReadNotes that help members assess how their CV, cover letter, and experience line up against specific opportunities.

read.careersinhr.com.au

Talent

The career positioning layer — helps members clarify the roles they want next, build a stronger private profile, and create better career language around strengths, motivations, experience, and future direction. Connects to aligned opportunity feeds from the broader Careers in HR market.

talent.careersinhr.com.au

Tenure

The private in-role reflection space — captures what's happening while someone is still in a role: fit, friction, confidence, growth, evidence, and lessons learned. Turns day-to-day work experience into clearer career insight over time.

tenure.careersinhr.com.au

Employers can submit HR opportunities into the ecosystem to appear in the HR Market Board, with optional promoted visibility across the wider Careers in HR network.

HR Search & Referral

Alongside the Careers in HR ecosystem, HeXTalent operates a small, deliberate HR search and referral practice. Most HR opportunities that come our way are matched to a trusted specialist recruiter from our network — one who genuinely fits the brief, the level, and the location. On a selective basis, we also take on HR search assignments directly.

  • Referral first. Curated introductions to specialist HR recruiters across Australia — no fee, no obligation, no volume game.
  • Direct search — selective. A handful of HR search assignments taken on personally each year, where the brief, the client, and the timing all align.
  • HR-only focus. Every referral partner and every direct engagement sits inside the HR discipline — nothing generalist, nothing off-piste.
