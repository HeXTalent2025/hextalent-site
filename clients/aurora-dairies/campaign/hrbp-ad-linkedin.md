### The role

HeXTalent is partnering with a well-established Australian business — currently in a period of significant organisational change — to appoint an experienced **Human Resources Business Partner** into their Melbourne CBD head office.

This is not a maintenance HR role. It's a build role. The HR function is deliberately early in its maturity as a standalone entity, and the GM HR is looking for a hands-on operational partner who can lift meaningful work off her plate, drive projects end-to-end, and help shape the shape of HR from here.

The organisation has a distributed workforce spanning corporate and operational teams across multiple Australian sites. This HRBP will sit in Melbourne CBD, working alongside an established network of regional HR Advisors as a senior peer — not managing them, but supporting and lifting the whole function.

---

### What you'll actually do

- **Operational HR** across the employee lifecycle for corporate and, where required, wider teams
- **Employee relations** — running investigations, drafting outcome letters, coaching leaders, providing oversight under the GM HR
- **Project delivery** — the biggest single component. Live projects include performance framework rollout, L&D framework build, HR system review, and process automation
- **Strategic contribution** — the GM HR is building a three-year HR vision; you'll help shape and deliver it
- **Workforce analytics** — turning metrics (turnover, absenteeism, headcount) into practical recommendations
- **Some travel to operational sites** — nothing weekly, but genuine willingness to jump in the car when it matters

---

### What we're looking for

**Essential:**
- HR qualification and demonstrated generalist experience with a real project delivery track record
- Strong employee relations knowledge (performance, conduct, grievances, investigations)
- Excellent project management and organisational skills
- Strong analytical and problem-solving capability
- Advanced Excel

**Strongly preferred:**
- Experience in operationally-oriented or distributed-workforce environments (any industry — FMCG, manufacturing, logistics, resources, agriculture, energy, transport, healthcare operations, etc.)
- Experience developing policies and business documentation from scratch
- Change management experience
- HR systems implementation or improvement experience

---

### Who thrives here

The GM HR describes the team as **"get shit done"** — a group of people who move at pace, trust each other to work flexibly, and value impact over process. That translates to:

- You'll bring genuine energy to a build environment
- You'll be comfortable stepping outside a rigid position description when the business needs it
- You'll be trusted to work flexibly — and expected to be in the office (2-3 days) when relationships and collaboration matter
- You'll be curious about the operation — not just the corporate side
- You'll be open-minded, adaptable, and innovative about how HR should work

If your comfort zone is a defined 9-5 in a polished, mature HR function — this isn't your role.

If your energy comes from making real impact in an environment where the canvas is wide open, this could be one of the more interesting HR roles going in Melbourne right now.
