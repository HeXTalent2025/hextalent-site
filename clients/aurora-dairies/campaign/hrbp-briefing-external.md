## About the business

Our client is a longstanding Australian dairy operations business with farms across Victoria and Tasmania. In late 2024 it was announced that the business would transition out of a broader asset-management structure to become a standalone entity — a transition completed in August 2025.

That transition has created a genuinely rare working environment. On the ground, it's a longstanding, operational business — cows are milked, feed programs are run, seasons dictate the rhythm. But at the head office level, it operates with a start-up mentality: newly-recruited teams, processes being built and improved, real autonomy to shape how the business functions from here.

The strategic focus over the next 12 months is on **data uplift** — bringing three different farm software systems into one coherent view, and using that data to drive better decisions on herd, feed, breeding, and workforce.

The business is currently between CEOs, with responsibilities being carried by three interim leaders including the General Manager – Human Resources. This has created significant autonomy for the HR function to move at pace on what matters.

## The HR function

The HR team is deliberately distributed to match the geography of the business:

- **Head office (Melbourne CBD)** — GM HR, HR Administration
- **Regional HR Advisors** — based in Warrnambool, Gippsland, and Tasmania
- **Regional WHS Advisors** — co-located with the HR Advisors
- **Regional admin support** — Mount Gambier, Gippsland

The business currently has:
- ~180 permanent employees
- ~140 casuals (with seasonal uplift during calving windows)
- ~50 corporate roles (of which ~40 are Melbourne-based)

The HR function is early in its maturity as a standalone entity. Strategy, performance framework, learning & development, employee value proposition, remuneration & benefits are all live areas that the HR team is actively building. This is a canvas rather than a maintenance role.

## The role

The HR Business Partner is the operational backbone and project engine of the HR function. It's a hands-on senior role that partners with leaders across the business to lift HR operations, deliver meaningful projects, and support the GM HR to move from operational execution into strategic partnering.

The successful candidate will be highly organised, solutions-focused, and comfortable in a hands-on operational environment. They'll manage multiple projects at once, drive process improvements, support leaders through organisational change, and keep HR operations efficient, compliant, and aligned with business priorities.

### The balance of the role

- **Operational HR** — ongoing advice and support across the employee lifecycle for corporate and, where required, regional teams
- **Employee relations** — running investigations, drafting outcome letters, coaching leaders. Volume ebbs and flows; the role provides oversight and delivery support to the GM HR without owning ER outright
- **Project delivery** — the biggest single component. Live and upcoming projects include on-farm accommodation standards, performance framework rollout, L&D framework build, HR system review, and process automation
- **Strategic contribution** — the GM HR is building a three-year vision for HR. The HRBP will play a real part in shaping and delivering it
- **Recruitment** — light. Regional HR Advisors handle their own regions; corporate hiring is occasional

### Key responsibilities

**HR operations**
- Provide operational HR advice across the employee lifecycle
- Manage ER matters (performance, conduct, grievances, investigations)
- Ensure compliance with employment legislation and industrial instruments
- Develop and maintain policies, procedures, and templates
- Support recruitment, onboarding, probation, and offboarding

**Project delivery**
- Lead HR projects end-to-end
- Support workforce change and consultation processes
- Contribute to HR systems and technology initiatives
- Manage workforce planning and organisational design projects
- Deliver initiatives that improve employee experience and operational efficiency

**Process improvement**
- Review and improve HR processes
- Develop tools, templates, and resources for leaders
- Identify opportunities to automate and streamline

**Stakeholder support**
- Partner with managers with practical, commercially-focused HR advice
- Coach leaders on people management and employment obligations
- Build collaborative relationships across the business

**Workforce planning and reporting**
- Analyse workforce data and prepare reports
- Monitor workforce metrics (turnover, vacancies, absenteeism, headcount)
- Recommend practical solutions to improve workforce performance

## Who we're looking for

### Essential
- Qualification in Human Resources or a related discipline
- Demonstrated generalist HR experience with real project delivery track record
- Strong employee relations knowledge
- Excellent project management and organisational skills
- Strong analytical and problem-solving capability

### Strongly preferred
- Experience in operational or distributed workforce environments (dairy, agriculture, FMCG, manufacturing, logistics, resources)
- Experience managing organisational change and workforce initiatives
- Experience developing policies and business documentation from scratch

- Workforce analytics and reporting capability

### Cultural fit
The GM HR describes the team as **"get shit done"** — a group of people who move at pace, trust each other to work flexibly, and value impact over process. The right person for this role will:

- Bring genuine energy to a build environment (this is not a maintenance role)
- Be comfortable stepping outside a rigid position description when the business needs it
- Value flexibility both ways — they'll be trusted to work from home when it's the right call, and expected to be in the office when relationships and collaboration matter
- Genuinely want to travel to farms — not because it's mandatory in the PD, but because they're curious about how the operation actually runs
- Be open-minded, adaptable, and innovative about how HR should work in this business
