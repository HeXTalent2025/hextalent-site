### The role

HeXTalent is partnering with a well-established Australian business — currently in a period of significant organisational change — to appoint an **HR Analyst & Systems Administrator** into their Melbourne CBD head office on a permanent 0.6 FTE basis (3 days per week).

This role has been shaped specifically as the **analytics-led seat** within the HR function. It's not an HR admin role with reporting on the side — it's a genuine workforce analytics and systems role, with the administration wrapped in.

The GM HR is looking to move HR decisions from *"we think this is happening"* to *"we know this is happening"* — and this role is the engine of that shift.

---

### What you'll actually do

**Workforce analytics & reporting (~40% of your time)**
- Build and maintain workforce dashboards and KPIs
- Analyse trends across headcount, turnover, absenteeism, workforce movements, and workforce capability
- Produce ad-hoc reporting for the GM HR and business leaders
- Provide data-driven insights that shape workforce planning and decisions
- Automate reporting where possible

**HRIS administration (~30%)**
- Administer the current HRIS — user access, workflows, configuration, and support
- Coordinate upgrades, releases, testing, and vendor coordination
- Provide first-line user support
- Maintain system documentation
- Play a real part in shaping a future HRIS decision (change is on the roadmap)

**Process improvement & project support (~15%)**
- Support HR projects through configuration, data analysis, and reporting
- UAT and testing for system enhancements
- Automation opportunities

**Data governance (~10%)**
- Data quality audits
- Integrity and privacy compliance
- Governance framework maintenance

**Some HR admin (~5%)**
- Corporate contracts only — regional teams handle their own regions

---

### What we're looking for

**Essential:**
- Qualification in HR, Information Systems, Business, Data Analytics, or a related discipline
- Experience with any HRIS platform — the business is genuinely technology-agnostic
- Experience in workforce reporting and data analysis
- **Advanced Excel** (this one's non-negotiable)
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent attention to detail
- Strong stakeholder engagement

**Strongly preferred:**
- Power BI or a similar dashboarding platform
- SQL, Power Query, Power Automate, or other data integration tools
- Experience implementing or upgrading HR systems
- Understanding of employment and payroll processes

We're intentionally open on background — could be an HR Analyst, HRIS Administrator, People & Culture Analyst, a Data Analyst with an HR crossover interest, or an HR Administrator with genuine analytical capability.

---

### Who thrives here

The GM HR describes the team as **"get shit done"** — a group of people who move at pace, trust each other to work flexibly, and value impact over process. Her framing on the technology piece is worth quoting:

> *"If you understand how a system works, it's just more so learning the intricacies of that new system."*

That translates to:

- You're genuinely curious about workforce data and what it can tell you
- You're not precious about specific platforms — you can work in whatever environment the business currently has, and help make it better
- You're comfortable in a build environment (this is not maintenance)
- You want to be in the office 2 of 3 days to build relationships and be close to the data
- You bring long-term thinking — this role is designed to grow with the business
