## Ad body

### Headline

**HR Analyst & Systems Administrator — Melbourne CBD**
*Part-time · 3 days per week · Circa $80k + super (pro-rata to 0.6)*

---

HeXTalent is partnering with a longstanding Australian business, currently in a period of meaningful organisational change, to appoint an **HR Analyst & Systems Administrator** into their Melbourne CBD head office on a permanent 0.6 FTE basis (3 days per week).

This is the **analytics-led seat** within the HR function. Not an HR admin role with reporting on the side — a genuine workforce analytics and systems role, with the administration wrapped in.

The GM HR wants to move HR decisions from *"we think this is happening"* to *"we know this is happening."* This role is the engine of that shift.

---

### What you'll do

- **Workforce analytics & reporting** — dashboards, KPIs, trend analysis, ad-hoc leader reporting
- **HRIS administration** — user access, workflows, first-line support, and helping shape a future HRIS decision
- **Data governance** — quality audits, integrity, privacy compliance
- **Process improvement & project support** — automation, UAT, HR project support

---

### What we're looking for

- Qualification in HR, Information Systems, Business, or Data Analytics
- Experience with any HRIS platform (the GM HR is genuinely technology-agnostic)
- Workforce reporting and data analysis experience
- **Advanced Excel** — non-negotiable
- Strong analytical mindset and attention to detail

**Highly regarded:** Power BI, SQL, Power Query, Power Automate; experience implementing or upgrading HR systems.

We're intentionally open on background — HR Analyst, HRIS Administrator, People & Culture Analyst, a Data Analyst with HR crossover, or an analytical HR Admin all welcome.

---

### Who thrives here

A **"get-shit-done"** team with high autonomy and a leadership style that trusts flexibility, rewards impact over process, and doesn't punish good-faith failure. Curious about workforce data, comfortable in imperfect systems, and thinking long-term about their next seat.

---

### Package

Circa **$80,000 FTE + super**, pro-rated to 0.6 (~$48k actual + super). 3 days per week, ideally 2 of them in office.

### Process

Screening & vetting with Jeff Teale (HeXTalent), then interviews with the GM HR and a senior executive stakeholder. References and pre-employment checks arranged by HeXTalent.
