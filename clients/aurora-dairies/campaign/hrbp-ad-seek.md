### About the opportunity

HeXTalent is partnering with a well-established Australian business — currently in a period of significant organisational change — to appoint an experienced **Human Resources Business Partner** into their Melbourne CBD head office.

The organisation has a distributed workforce spanning corporate and operational teams across multiple Australian sites. It sits in a unique moment: a longstanding, well-established operation combined with a genuinely start-up mentality at head office as processes, systems, and strategy are built out for the next chapter.

The HR function is deliberately early in its maturity. Rather than inheriting rhythm and structure, the successful HRBP will help shape it — with the encouragement, trust, and autonomy of a supportive GM HR who believes in agnostic thinking, adaptability, and impact.

This is not a maintenance HR role. It's a build role — for someone who wants to make a mark.

---

### About the role

Reporting to the General Manager – Human Resources, the HRBP will act as the operational backbone and project engine of the HR function. The role sits alongside an existing network of regional HR Advisors as a senior peer — not managing them, but supporting and lifting the whole function.

**Key responsibilities:**

**HR Operations**
- Providing operational HR advice and support to leaders across the employee lifecycle
- Managing employee relations matters including performance, conduct, grievances, and investigations
- Ensuring compliance with employment legislation, industrial instruments, and organisational policies
- Developing and maintaining HR policies, procedures, templates, and documentation
- Supporting recruitment, onboarding, probation, and offboarding activities

**Project Delivery**
- Leading and delivering HR projects from planning through to implementation and evaluation
- Supporting the GM HR with workforce change initiatives and consultation processes
- Supporting implementation of new HR systems, technology, and process improvements
- Managing workforce planning and organisational design projects
- Delivering initiatives that improve employee experience, operational efficiency, and workforce capability

**Process Improvement**
- Reviewing and improving HR processes to increase efficiency and consistency
- Developing tools, templates, and resources to support leaders
- Identifying opportunities to automate and streamline

**Stakeholder Support**
- Partnering with managers to provide practical, commercially focused HR advice
- Coaching leaders on people management practices and employment obligations
- Supporting change management activities

**Workforce Planning and Reporting**
- Analysing workforce data and preparing reports for operational decision-making
- Monitoring workforce metrics including turnover, vacancies, absenteeism, and headcount
- Identifying trends and recommending practical solutions
- Supporting budgeting and establishment management activities

The role involves some travel to operational sites away from Melbourne. Openness to that rhythm matters more than the frequency.
