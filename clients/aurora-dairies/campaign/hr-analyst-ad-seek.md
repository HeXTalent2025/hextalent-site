### About the opportunity

HeXTalent is partnering with a well-established Australian business — currently in a period of significant organisational change — to appoint an experienced **HR Analyst & Systems Administrator** into their Melbourne CBD head office on a permanent 0.6 FTE basis (3 days per week).

This role has been shaped as the **analytics-led seat** within the HR function — not an HR admin role with reporting on the side, but a genuine workforce analytics and systems role, with the administration wrapped in.

The GM HR is looking to move HR decisions from *"we think this is happening"* to *"we know this is happening."* Data uplift is a key business priority; this role plays that role inside the HR function.

---

### About the role

Reporting to the General Manager – Human Resources, this role owns the HR systems and workforce data function.

**Key responsibilities:**

**HR Systems Administration**
- Administer and maintain the organisation's HRIS and associated people systems
- Manage user access, security roles, workflows, and system configuration
- Coordinate system upgrades, testing, releases, and vendor updates
- Troubleshoot system issues and provide first-line user support
- Develop and maintain system documentation, user guides, and SOPs
- Identify and implement opportunities to improve system functionality
- Support implementation of new HR technologies
- Provide technical HRIS support to the Payroll team

**Workforce Analytics and Reporting**
- Develop and maintain workforce dashboards, reports, and KPIs
- Produce ad hoc workforce reporting for leaders
- Analyse workforce trends relating to headcount, turnover, recruitment, absenteeism, diversity, and workforce capability
- Provide data-driven insights to support workforce planning and business decisions
- Develop automated reporting solutions where possible

**Data Management and Governance**
- Ensure the accuracy, integrity, and security of workforce data
- Conduct regular data quality audits and implement corrective actions
- Maintain data governance standards and reporting frameworks
- Ensure compliance with privacy legislation

**Process Improvement**
- Review HR processes and identify opportunities for automation
- Lead initiatives to improve HR system efficiency and reporting capability
- Develop templates, workflows, and reporting solutions
- Participate in organisational projects involving workforce systems

**Project Support**
- Support HR projects through system configuration, data analysis, and reporting
- Assist with workforce planning and system implementation
- Coordinate testing and user acceptance activities

**Stakeholder Support**
- Provide technical advice and training to HR and business users
- Partner with leaders to understand reporting and system requirements
- Liaise with IT & Business Services, system vendors, and external providers
