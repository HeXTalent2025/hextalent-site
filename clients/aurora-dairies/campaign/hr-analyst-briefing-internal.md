## 3. The Role in Detail

### Purpose
Own the HR systems and data function for Aurora Dairies. Bring genuine analytics capability that the current setup lacks, provide the workforce insight layer the GM HR needs to make informed decisions, and act as a system administrator who can navigate ELMO, contribute to a future system change, and keep data quality tight.

### Balance
Jenna is deliberately keen for this role to have a **real analytics lean** — not just an admin role with reporting on the side. Rough split:

- **HRIS administration & configuration** (~30%) — ELMO administration, user access, workflows, releases, troubleshooting, first-line user support, documentation
- **Workforce analytics & reporting** (~40%) — dashboards, workforce KPIs, ad-hoc reporting for leaders, turnover analysis, headcount by farm, movement metrics, workforce trends
- **Data management & governance** (~10%) — data quality audits, integrity work, privacy compliance
- **Process improvement & project support** (~15%) — HR system enhancements, project support, testing, UAT
- **Some HR administration** (~5%) — corporate contracts only, minimal (regional advisors handle their regions)

### Key stakeholders
- **Jenna** — direct manager, primary consumer of insights
- **IT team** — critical partner, building broader business dashboards; this role will interface heavily to leverage IT-built infrastructure for HR
- **Regional HR Advisors** — providing tools and insights they can use with managers on the ground
- **Payroll team** — technical HRIS support to help them, given the system's shortcomings
- **Finance** — occasional collaboration on workforce cost analysis

### Success measures (Jenna is flexible on specifics)
- **30 days:** Fully across the ELMO landscape, understanding of data flows, meeting all stakeholders
- **60 days:** First dashboards/insights in production; initial data quality audit completed
- **90 days:** Own the analytics rhythm; identifying and delivering meaningful improvements
- **12 months:** Analytics function is genuinely valuable; data quality has measurably improved; role is set up to lead any future system change

## 4. Candidate Profile

### Non-negotiables
- **Advanced Excel** — Jenna's quote: *"you can't survive in this world without Excel"*
- **Fundamental understanding of HR systems** — the specific platform matters less than the pattern recognition
- **Analytical capability** — someone who can look at workforce data and draw genuine insight, not just produce reports
- **Attitude** — get-things-done mindset, not rigid about role boundaries
- **Willingness to be in the office 2 of 3 days** to build relationships
- **Long-term commitment** — role is designed to grow with the business

### Strongly preferred
- **Power BI or Tableau** experience (dashboard-building capability)
- Experience with **any HRIS platform** — SuccessFactors, Workday, Employment Hero, ELMO, iChris, similar
- **Data quality/integrity** background
- Workforce reporting and analytics experience
- Understanding of employment and payroll processes

### Also welcomed
- Power Query, Power Automate, SQL — nice-to-haves
- Experience implementing or upgrading HR systems
- Direct system administrator experience

### Deal-breakers
- Pure administrator background with no analytical capability
- Rigid mindset — wanting a defined 9-5 with clear PD boundaries
- Preciousness about specific systems / unwilling to work in ELMO because it's imperfect

### Cultural fit
Same tests as the HRBP — get-shit-done, mutual trust on flexibility, comfortable with imperfect systems, willingness to build and improve.

### Candidate pool considerations
Jenna is **technology-agnostic and attitude-first**. Her exact framing: *"if you understand how a system works, it's just more so learning the intricacies of that new system."*

This opens the pool meaningfully. Consider candidates from:
- HR Analyst / HR Systems backgrounds (obvious core pool)
- Data Analyst backgrounds with HR crossover interest
- HR Administrator with genuine analytical capability
- People & Culture Analyst
- HRIS Analyst / HRIS Administrator
- Workforce Planning Analyst
