## About the business

Our client is a longstanding Australian dairy operations business with farms across Victoria and Tasmania. In late 2024 it was announced that the business would transition out of a broader asset-management structure to become a standalone entity — a transition completed in August 2025.

On the ground, the business is BAU: cows milked, feed programs run, seasons dictate the rhythm. At the head office level, it operates with a start-up mentality — newly-recruited teams, processes being built and refined, real autonomy to shape how the business functions from here.

The strategic focus over the next 12 months is on **data uplift** — bringing multiple farm software systems into one coherent view, and using that data to drive better decisions on herd management, feed programs, breeding, and workforce planning. A new Chief Technical Services Officer is leading that work.

The business is currently between CEOs, with responsibilities being carried by three interim leaders including the General Manager – Human Resources. This has created significant autonomy for the HR function to move at pace on what matters.

The HR team is deliberately distributed to match the geography of the business:

## The role

This role has been shaped specifically as the analytics-led seat within the HR function. Its job is to bring genuine workforce insight into HR decision-making, own and improve the HR systems environment, and provide the reporting and data foundation the business needs to move from gut-feel to insight-led people decisions.

The GM HR wants to move HR decisions from "we think this is happening" to "we know this is happening" — this role is the engine of that shift.

### The balance of the role

- **Workforce analytics & reporting** (~40%) — dashboards, workforce KPIs, ad-hoc reporting for leaders, turnover, headcount by farm, movement metrics, workforce trend analysis
- **HRIS administration & configuration** (~30%) — system administration, user access, workflows, releases, troubleshooting, first-line user support, documentation
- **Process improvement & project support** (~15%) — system enhancements, HR project support, testing and UAT
- **Data management & governance** (~10%) — data quality audits, integrity work, privacy compliance
- **Some HR administration** (~5%) — corporate contracts only (regional advisors handle their regions)

### Key responsibilities

**HR systems administration**
- Administer and maintain the organisation's HRIS
- Manage user access, security, workflows, and system configuration
- Coordinate system upgrades, testing, releases, and vendor updates
- Troubleshoot system issues and provide first-line user support
- Develop and maintain system documentation
- Support the implementation of new HR technologies

**Workforce analytics and reporting**
- Develop and maintain workforce dashboards, reports, and KPIs
- Analyse workforce trends across headcount, turnover, recruitment, absenteeism, and workforce capability
- Provide data-driven insights to support workforce planning and business decisions
- Develop automated reporting solutions where possible

**Data management and governance**
- Ensure accuracy, integrity, and security of workforce data
- Conduct regular data quality audits
- Maintain data governance standards
- Ensure privacy compliance

**Process improvement**
- Review HR processes for automation and continuous improvement
- Lead initiatives to improve HR system efficiency and reporting
- Develop templates, workflows, and reporting solutions

**Stakeholder support**
- Provide technical HRIS support to the Payroll team
- Provide technical advice and training to HR and business users
- Partner with leaders to understand reporting and system requirements
- Liaise with IT, system vendors, and external providers

### Key stakeholders
- GM HR (direct manager and primary consumer of insights)
- IT & Business Services (critical partner — the business's broader data infrastructure is being built and this role connects HR into it)
- Regional HR Advisors (providing tools they can use with managers on the ground)
- Payroll team (technical HRIS support)
- Finance (occasional collaboration on workforce cost analysis)

- Experience in workforce reporting and data analysis

### Cultural fit
The GM HR describes the team as **"get shit done"** — a group of people who move at pace, trust each other to work flexibly, and value impact over process. She's technology-agnostic and attitude-first:

> "If you understand how a system works, it's just more so learning the intricacies of that new system."

The right person for this role will:
- Be genuinely curious about workforce data and what it can tell you
- Not be precious about specific platforms — able to work in whatever environment the business currently has
- Be comfortable in a build environment (this is not a maintenance role)
- Be open-minded, adaptable, and willing to help shape better ways of working
- Want to be in the office 2 of 3 days to build relationships and be close to the data
